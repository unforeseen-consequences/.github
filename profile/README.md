## 📡 What this org is

**Unforeseen Consequences** is a research + engineering lab for building at the edge of blockchain infrastructure.

We focus on:

- **Secure signing** (remote signers, policy gates, audit trails)
- **Validator / node tooling** (ops, automation, observability, resilience)
- **Protocol experimentation** (new ideas, controlled chaos, hard lessons)
- **Hardening & reliability** (because incidents don’t care about intentions)

## 🧰 Engineering principles

- **Security first** (assume hostile networks, minimize trust)
- **Deterministic by design** (repeatable builds, reproducible behavior)
- **Observable systems** (metrics, traces, logs)
- **Simple > clever** (clever breaks at 3AM)
- **Fail safe** (when it fails, it fails _closed_)

---

## 🔒 Remote signing philosophy

Keys are anomalous materials:

- Keep them **isolated**
- Put **policy** between the world and the key
- Make signing **auditable**
- Make misuse **hard**
- Make recovery **possible**
